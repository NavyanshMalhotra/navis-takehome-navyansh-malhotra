CONTEXT & WEALTH MANAGEMENT GRAIN:
In wealth management, client data is grouped at the HOUSEHOLD level. A household groups people (clients), money (custodian accounts), and historical interactions (reviews, calls).
Unstructured notes frequently contain crucial facts such as:
1. Spousal and family relationships (e.g., "Wife of Robert Chen — same household", "Joint account with husband").

3. Custodian and currency details (e.g., "Account held with a Swiss custodian in francs", "Lombard Intl account in euros").
4. Historical lineage and acquisition tags (e.g., "Came over in Harborline acquisition", "Left us in 2023, winding down").
5. Advisor relationships, unassigned accounts, or departures (e.g., "A. Novak left last year", "Never got a proper advisor of record").

EXTRACTION INSTRUCTIONS:
- Extract all verifiable relationship facts and operational constraints.
- Cite the EXACT source snippet, file name, and line context for every extracted fact.
